I retained my knowledge of PC building from the previous build, but this time I
had to refine assembly techniques to adapt to the challenge of a smaller build.
Using [pcpartpicker](https://pcpartpicker.com), I planned out the components to
get that are compatible and powerful while maintaining decent temperatures (to
avoid overheating in a smaller form factor). The build runs on Intel LGA 1851
socket (Intel Core Ultra 200s Arrow Lake chips), using an Intel Core Ultra 7 265KF
CPU, 32 GB DDR5 ram (6400 MT/s), 2 TB SSD storage, and an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070
as the GPU.
